Description

Villa Jasmine offers two bedrooms, a fully equipped kitchen combined with a living room with TV and two bathrooms. Each room is equipped with air conditioning. Outside the villa you will find an infinity pool and a new barbecue area and, most importantly, a breathtaking view of Souda Bay. The villa is 12 minutes walk from the tourist village of Almyrida, where you will find a beach line and many taverns, shops and bars.
